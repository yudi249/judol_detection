Online gambling refers to any form of gambling activity conducted over the internet, using devices such as mobile
phones, laptops or tablets [5]. Compared to conventional gambling, online gambling has characteristics such as high

control. In the context of social media, this practice often appears in the form of hidden promotions in the comments
section, as is rampant on the YouTube platform.
Director of the Communication and Information System Security Research Center (CISSReC), Pratama Persadha,
stated that YouTube's comment section is now the main target for spreading online gambling spam due to the high
level of user engagement on the platform [6]. These comments generally contain links or invitations to join certain
gambling sites with the lure of huge profits and are often spread using automated bots or fake accounts to reach more
users at low cost. In response to this phenomenon, cybersecurity expert Alfons Tanujaya suggested that content
creators moderate or filter the comment section [6]. However, this step can be inefficient when the number of
comments reaches tens of thousands because it requires large resources and a lot of time. Given these limitations, if
the trend of spreading online gambling promotions through YouTube comments is not addressed promptly, the impact
may become increasingly widespread and difficult to contain. This condition highlights the urgency of developing an
automatic detection system that can accurately and efficiently identify online gambling promotion content.
Detection of harmful content on social media, such as hate speech, hoaxes, and online gambling promotions, is
mostly done with Natural Language Processing (NLP) approaches. This technology continues to grow along with the
increasing need for automated surveillance systems on social media. Various methods have been applied in previous
studies, ranging from conventional machine learning to deep learning-based models. Detection of Indonesian online

extractor with MBERT as a classifier for multilabel classification of toxic comments in Indonesian, resulting in an F1-

After the comment data was collected, 10% of the total dataset was manually labeled into two classes, which are
online gambling Promotion and Non-Promotion. The labeling process was carried out by the first author with several
indicators. A comment is categorized as Promotion if it contains the name of online gambling site which generally
consists of a combination of letters at the beginning and numbers at the end, invitations to play like “main” (play),
“gampang” (easy), “coba” (try), or the use of terms that are closely related to online gambling, such as “jepe”
(jackpot), “nagih” (addict), or “slot” (refers to online gambling). Meanwhile, comments that have no indication of
online gambling promotion are categorized as Non-Promotion. Simple labeling guidelines were established to
maintain consistency during annotation and ambiguous cases were manually reviewed. To ensure accuracy, all labeled
data will be double-checked by annotators to reduce subjectivity and minimize labeling errors. This annotated subset
serves as the initial supervised dataset used to train a baseline classification model.
2.2. Data Preprocessing
Data preprocessing is a series of steps used to clean, integrate, transform, and reduce data before it is used in the
model training process [16]. This stage is crucial because the use of data that has not been properly prepared can result
in erroneous information and suboptimal model performance [17]. Moreover, the quality of the data will be further
degraded if there is a lot of meaningless information or noise in it [18]. Therefore, understanding and applying the
right preprocessing procedure according to the context of the case study is very important to ensure the quality of the
data used.
In this research, comment data from YouTube is processed through a series of text cleaning stages. Fig. 2 illustrates
the results of the data preprocessing applied in this study. These stages include unicode character conversion, removal
of emojis, URLs, foreign characters, and symbols, as well as the merging of gambling promotion keywords that are
separated by spaces. The text is then normalized to lowercase, followed by tokenization and stemming to return words
to their base forms. An unslang process is also applied using a simple slang dictionary, which replaces abbreviated
forms such as “ad” with “ada” or “adlh” with “adalah.” Finally, stop word removal is performed to eliminate non-
informative words. All preprocessing steps are applied consistently to ensure high-quality input for model training.
After preprocessing, the dataset is split into training, validation, and test sets using a 7:1,5:1,5 ratio.

2.3. Descriptive Analysis
In the context of NLP, descriptive analysis is used to explore word distribution and understand language patterns
contained in a text corpus [19]. This stage is important as an initial step in identifying keywords that are often used,
especially in comments containing online gambling promotions. One technique that is often done in descriptive
analysis of text data is through word cloud visualization. Word cloud is a text visualization used to summarize the
content of a document into a visual representation of the most important words [20]. The size of each word in the
word cloud is scaled proportionally based on its frequency in the text so that words that appear more frequently will
be displayed in a larger size. This research uses word cloud visualization to intuitively display the frequency of word
occurrence. The results of the word cloud provide an initial insight into the language strategies used by the
perpetrators, such as the use of site names, promotional invitations, and gambling terms.
